# Quantum States, Superposition, and Interference

## Quantum States & Superposition
A quantum state |ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩ represents a normalized state vector in Hilbert space where |α|² + |β|² = 1.
Superposition allows a quantum system to process combinations of basis states simultaneously.

## Quantum Interference
Quantum interference occurs when probability amplitudes combine constructively or destructively.
Applying a Hadamard gate H creates equal superposition (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2. A second H gate causes constructive interference for |0⟩ and destructive interference for |1⟩, returning the state back to |0⟩.

# Quantum Circuits and Algorithms

## Quantum Circuits
A quantum circuit is a computational model consisting of sequences of quantum gates, measurements, and resets acting on qubits.
Circuit execution maps an initial state (typically |00...0⟩) to a final statevector through unitary matrix multiplications.

## Quantum Algorithms
Quantum algorithms exploit quantum superposition, entanglement, and interference to solve problems faster than classical algorithms:
- Deutsch-Jozsa: Determines if a function is constant or balanced in 1 query.
- Bernstein-Vazirani: Finds a hidden bitstring s in 1 query.
- Grover's Algorithm: Unstructured search in O(√N) queries.
